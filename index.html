Our Vision, Mission, and Commitment

Partnership Synergy

NASPA Ghana & Zeus Atlas Ltd

The National Service Personnel Association (NASPA) is the official body representing over 100,000 service graduates across Ghana. In response to youth employment challenges, NASPA partnered with Zeus Atlas Limited—a leader in tech innovation, digital capacity development, and sustainable agriculture.

Together, our objective is to turn the mandatory service period into an incubation phase for tech innovators, smart farmers, and project leaders.

Core Strategic Focus Areas
  • Elevating digital competency & modern AI skills.
  • Introducing precision agriculture and modernized agritech.
  • Raising leadership, agility, and legal awareness.
  • Creating direct pathways to overseas study and scholarship options.

Privacy Policy

How we handle your information

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal data like your name, National Service Scheme (NSS) registration number, primary telephone contact, email address, and service deployment details when you apply to the NASPA Skills Training Program.

2. How We Use Your Data

This information is used strictly to verify your national service status, schedule your training sessions, provide communications from instructors, and issue certificates of completion.

3. Third-Party Sharing

Your personal data is never sold or shared with third parties for commercial use. Information is only shared between NASPA Ghana and Zeus Atlas Limited to support your participation in the program.

Terms & Conditions

Program Guidelines & Participation Rules

1. Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must be currently serving National Service Personnel in Ghana with a valid NSS Number. Providing inaccurate details may result in removal from the program.

2. Attendance Policy

To receive a certificate of completion, participants must attend at least 80% of live classes, complete practical assignments, and pass final competency tests.

3. Intellectual Property

All learning resources, software tool instructions, data simulators, and Agri-tech guides are the shared intellectual property of Zeus Atlas and NASPA Ghana. Distributing materials commercially is strictly prohibited.
